1. Subscription Overview

Author's Space is a subscription-based book listing service offered by Yellow Feather Books. By subscribing, authors gain the ability to submit books for listing on yellowfeatherbookstore.in, our Instagram page, and the Yellow Feather mobile app.

🔄 Author subscription plans are recurring monthly subscriptions. Your plan will automatically renew each month and your account will be charged until you cancel. You can cancel at any time from your Author's Space dashboard.

3. Billing & Auto-Renewal

Your subscription begins on the date of your first payment and renews automatically every 30 days. Charges will be applied to the payment method you authorise through Razorpay, our payment processor. You will receive an invoice by email after each successful charge.

If a renewal payment fails, your subscription may be suspended until payment is successfully completed.

4. Book Submission Quota

Each plan includes a fixed number of book submissions per billing month. Unused submissions do not carry forward to the next month. Additional submission slots can be purchased as top-up packs from your Author's Space dashboard.

5. Plan Changes & Upgrades

You may upgrade your plan at any time. On upgrade, your existing subscription will be cancelled immediately and a new subscription will begin at the higher plan rate. Downgrading is not supported mid-cycle; you may cancel and re-subscribe at the lower tier at the start of the next cycle.

6. Cancellation

You may cancel your subscription at any time from the Author's Space dashboard. Upon cancellation:

  • No further charges will be made after the current billing cycle ends
  • Your existing book listings will remain active until the end of the paid period
  • You will lose the ability to submit new books once the cycle ends
  • You can re-subscribe at any time to restore submission access

7. Refund Policy

Monthly subscription fees are non-refundable once a billing cycle has commenced. If you believe you have been charged in error, please contact us at bookstore@yellowfeatherbooks.com within 7 days of the charge, and we will investigate promptly.

8. Book Listing Standards

All book submissions are subject to review by our editorial team. Yellow Feather Books reserves the right to reject or remove any submission that:

  • Contains inappropriate, offensive, or legally problematic content
  • Violates any third-party intellectual property rights
  • Is submitted with inaccurate or misleading information

Rejection of a submission does not entitle the author to a refund of the monthly fee.

9. Account Responsibility

Your Author's Space account is personal and non-transferable. You are responsible for all activity carried out under your account. Do not share your login credentials with others.
