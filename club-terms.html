The Yellow Feather Book Club is an exclusive reader community offered by Yellow Feather Books. Membership grants access to member-only benefits for a fixed period of one (1) year from the date of payment.

📅 Your membership is valid for 12 months from the date of joining. It does not auto-renew. When your membership period ends, you will automatically revert to a guest account — no charges will be made without your explicit action.

The current membership fee is ₹99 (inclusive of all taxes). This is a one-time payment for the full 12-month membership period. There are no hidden charges or recurring billing cycles.

If you choose not to renew, your account will automatically revert to a standard guest account. Your order history and account details will be retained. You may rejoin the Book Club at any time in the future.
